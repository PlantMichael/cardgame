## Outcome and decisions

Replace the proposed Pa'tael AI work with a ranked playtest: before either mulligan, player two chooses (a) four drawn cards plus a Coin, (b) five drawn cards total, or (c) four drawn cards plus a vanilla 1/1 token. Coin is a zero-cost card granting one additional usable mana for the turn it is played, without permanent ramp. Token starts on the battlefield and cannot attack on its owner's first turn; it readies on its owner's second turn. First player still starts with three cards; normal mana/draw progression remains. Operator explicitly selected four cards for coin/token and application to human ranked and normal ranked-AI fallback. Any match involving Io, Callisto, Europa or Ganymede retains baseline 3/4 opening with no choice. No data wipe, Pa'tael improvement, deck balancing, non-ranked/campaign/legacy PvP change or bot policy redesign.

Coin is granted after its owner's mulligan, preserving four exchangeable deck cards and preventing swapping the bonus into the deck. Five-card option draws the fifth before mulligan. Token is present before mulligan and ordinary combat/effects can interact with it. Choice cannot be declined; timeout defaults to five cards, then opens an independent normal mulligan clock (existing 90s). AI fallback chooses among legal options with a small hand-curve heuristic, then uses existing play policy for Coin. This is an implementation policy, not a balance claim.
