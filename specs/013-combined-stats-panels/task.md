## Additive public contract

Keep mode=ranked_ai (legacy default) and authoritative_pvp unchanged. Add explicit mode=all: load both existing eligible sources, enforce a combined 50,000-source-match ceiling, resolve latest rules across the union, and pool every AI level/policy (including unknown policy) within those rules. AI filters are irrelevant and return 400 in all/PvP modes. Keep date bounds, validation, timeouts, cache/failure behavior, publication allowlist and 4 MiB limit. Missing PvP tables mean AI-only combined results, not failure; unavailable DB/source means 503, never a partial successful pool.

For all, role/opponentRole are combined, deck identity remains composition hash, methodology.rolesSeparated=false and source=mixed. Legacy modes retain human/ai identities, rolesSeparated=true and existing provenance. Both perspectives contribute; an identical-list mirror yields two appearances, one win and one loss in its single bucket. Physical games remains the source-row count; deck appearances sum to twice games and wins sum to games. Distinct testers union source participant sets; no identities escape. Available AI filter arrays are empty for all/PvP and filters.aiVersion/aiLevel are null.

Add a playStats envelope to snapshots: totalSeats=2*games, trackedSeats (seats with some valid actual-play recording), completeSeats (new full-play recording), and cards=[{id,plays}] for published cards with recorded counts. Counts include actual accepted plays, including real replays/generated non-token card plays; mere draw, attack, transformation or summon is not a play. Use new full counters when present. Historical PvP deck_cards played_copies may supply known partial counts, explicitly not complete coverage; absent/invalid metadata never becomes an invented zero. Malformed optional stored telemetry cannot discard an otherwise eligible game's deck results. Catalog validates/projects the envelope, integer bounds, unique published IDs and coverage relationships. For old upstream filtered-mode snapshots lacking the additive envelope, project unknown coverage. Do not expose per-seat facts or IDs.

Cards derive game-weighted starting-deck metrics from selected deck rows: total deck appearances=sum(matches); appearances containing card=sum(matches for each containing list); in-decks fraction=containing/total; average copies=sum(matches*copies)/containing; deck win rate=sum(wins for containing lists)/containing. A card counted twice in a list still has one inclusion per appearance. Actual plays are independent of inclusion: a generated card can have plays without starting-deck appearances. With no recorded play seats, Times played is unavailable, not zero; with partial recording, show known counts with a clear coverage note. Empty results and unavailable requests remain distinct. Expose sample sizes through help/details and small-sample warnings. No claim these pooled results prove causal card power.
